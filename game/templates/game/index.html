How to Play

  • Click a piece to select it
  • Click a valid hex to move
  • After moving, click "Rotate Piece" to change orientation
  • ⚔️ Warrior: moves 1-3 hexes forward
  • 🔍 Scout: moves 1 hex forward, left, or right
  • Capture opponent pieces by moving onto them
